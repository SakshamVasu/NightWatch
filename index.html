ABOUT THE ANALYSIS

Read the evidence carefully

Nightwatch formats the contents of your Nmap report. It does not run a scan or verify the target independently.

  • Reported finding means the scan output explicitly labels a result or vulnerability.
  • Version-associated means a service version is visible; that alone does not establish that it is vulnerable.
  • Unparsed output remains available in the raw report for your review.

Files are parsed locally in this browser and are never sent to a server.
